# Jumanly · English texts of the public site

English version of the public site texts (`textes/textes-application.md`, parts 01, 05 and 10).
Same keys as French: `pnpm messages` writes `messages/en.json`, laid over French at run time. A key
missing here keeps its French text. Variables (`{price}`, `{count, plural, …}`) must stay the same.

Contact by e-mail only, never a phone number. Prices come from the database (`{price}`).
